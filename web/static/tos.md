## 2. Description of Service

Tippy is a community economy on the Base blockchain. It provides:
- USDC tipping, rain and a custodial balance between Telegram users
- Prediction markets with an LMSR automated market maker
- Pool bets (parimutuel), settled by the pool creator
- Creator tokens and paywalled posts, plus paid channel subscriptions
- On-chain outcome markets (ERC-1155) traded through a smart account
- x402 payment endpoints for AI agents
- An AI assistant that answers from ledger data and, when configured, from a third-party language model

The Service is experimental software. Features may change, be suspended or be withdrawn.
