Features

Money, markets and content in one chat

Only the official USDC contract sits in the hot path; everything else is instant internal accounting backed by public proof of reserves. No custom contracts where they are not needed.

Instant tips, zero gas

/tip 5 @nick or reply to a message; /rain scatters USDC across the group and emoji reactions tip the author. An ERC-4337 paymaster sponsors the gas.

LMSR prediction markets

Live odds that move with demand on Hanson's LMSR, Decimal-exact. Buy and sell shares any time before resolution; solvency is proven by property tests along aggressive trading paths.

Wallets & donations

Custodial wallet with seed export/import, a /donate page with QR, auto-credited deposits and on-chain withdrawals staged behind an explicit Confirm tap.

Paywalls & x402

Sell posts and paid channel access for USDC. The x402 HTTP 402 handshake lets AI agents pay on-chain: invoice, pay, replay-proof credit.

Autonomous agent

Crypto news filtered by an LLM becomes markets, bets and paywall posts — every action EAS-attested on Base. Fail-closed: spend caps live in code, never in the prompt.

Proof of reserves

A public dashboard shows liabilities against on-chain USDC, live stats, markets and leaderboards. The TipBotVault treasury makes every payout auditable on Basescan.

Run

Bot and dashboard in one process

aiogram bot, FastAPI dashboard and a web3 layer over Base, with Solidity contracts tested on a local EVM. The source is published for audit; deployment stays with the team.
